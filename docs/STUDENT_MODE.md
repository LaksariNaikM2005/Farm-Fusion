# 🎓 Student Mode & The Tripartite Ecosystem

## Concept: Farmer ↔ Student ↔ Expert Collaboration
Farm Fusion 2.0 creates a unique academic bridge where real-world agricultural problems submitted by farmers become educational case studies for agriculture and veterinary undergraduate students.

```
[ Farmer Submits Crop/Animal Problem ]
                 ↓
[ Problem Added to Academic Case Study Pool ]
                 ↓
[ Agriculture Student Studies Symptoms & Literature ]
                 ↓
[ Student Submits Diagnostic Hypothesis & Action Plan ]
                 ↓
[ Certified Scientist / Expert Reviews & Validates ]
                 ↓
[ Verified Solution Delivered to Farmer & Archived in Knowledge Base ]
```

---

## Student Features
1. **ICAR Curriculum Courseware**: Standardized study modules in Agronomy, Plant Pathology, Soil Science, and Animal Husbandry with key exam takeaways.
2. **Competitive MCQ Quiz Arena**: Timed tests with instant score computation, performance statistics, and scientific explanations referenced to academic textbooks.
3. **Real-World Case Study Analysis**: Practical diagnostic exercises bridging classroom theory and field realities.
