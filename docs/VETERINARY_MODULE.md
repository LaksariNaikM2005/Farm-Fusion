# 🐾 Veterinary & Animal Husbandry Module

## Mission
To provide smallholder and mixed-crop farmers with structured livestock management, automated vaccination tracking, and safety-guided animal symptom triage without replacing certified veterinary doctors.

---

## Capabilities
1. **Livestock Digital Registry**: Track individual cattle, buffaloes, goats, sheep, and poultry with ear-tag identification, age, breed, lactation status, and daily milk yields.
2. **Vaccination & Healthcare Timeline**:
   - Bi-annual Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) booster tracking.
   - Pre-monsoon Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) and Black Quarter (BQ) scheduling.
   - Regular deworming calendars.
3. **AI Safety Symptom Triage**:
   - Compares observed clinical symptoms against ICAR-IVRI compendiums.
   - Categorizes urgency (`Emergency`, `High`, `Moderate`).
   - Generates immediate first-aid supportive protocols while mandating veterinary doctor escalation.
